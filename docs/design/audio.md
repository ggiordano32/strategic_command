### Audio (as built, 2026-10-10)

## DESIGN paragraph (Audio)
Sound is view-only: nothing in `sim/` or `campaign/` knows about it and nothing
is hashed. `game/audio.gd` (autoload `Audio`, reached through the static
helpers of `preload("res://game/audio.gd")`) holds a pool of 14 sample
players on buses Master <- Effects / Ambient (Effects <- five pan buses with
a Panner each). The battle reads sim counters and states after each tick
(melee `stat_attacks`, `stat_impacts`, `stat_shots`, `stat_bolts` /
`stat_stones`, `stat_crush` / `stat_amok`, `stat_released`, `u_state`
transitions, `u_stair`, gate `g_hp` / `g_state`, artillery landings from the
`pr_*` buckets, `u_burn` for the fire loop) and plays at most one clip per
event bucket per 100 ms (CAPS in audio.gd), at a volume and pan from the
event's distance and side from the camera centre. The overworld plays on
`Kit.button` clicks, orders placed / refused, end of turn, turn resolved,
battle pending (also the online notification in `OnlineCampaign._adopt`),
and the chime for treaty / trade / gift orders. Ambient beds (2026-10-22, long
and quiet so they do not read as a loop): the map bed is 48 s of two surf layers on ~7 s and ~11 s swells
(random-walk periods) over a slow low wind, with 2-3 gulls and one far rigging creak placed at random and never
within 6 s of the loop point; the battle bed is 48 s of low wind, a very slow distant rumble and a far crow or gull.
Both: equal-power 2 s crossfade at the loop point, peak -12 dBFS, RMS about -28 dBFS; `fire_loop` is 12 s of
randomised crackle. All three are generated by `tools/make_sounds.py`. Playback (2026-10-22): the clips are QOA-compressed on import, so the loop end is
set from `get_length() * mix_rate` (the first version used `data.size() / 2`, a fraction of the length: the bed wrapped
at 9.7 s and cut off); the `.import` files carry `edit/loop_mode=2` (forward; 1 is Disabled in this importer), and
`Audio.Looper` plays each loop on two players that cross-fade (equal power, cos / sin) over the last 2 s (1 s for
the fire) and swap, so the wrap does not depend on the backend. The baked 2 s crossfade is kept: the file's head is
constant power on its own, so the player fade adds no dip.
Web: nothing plays until the first tap / click / key. Volumes
(master 80 / effects 100 / ambient 35 %) and mute are in
`user://settings.cfg [audio]`, set from the home page's sound row.
Clips are generated by `tools/make_sounds.py` (numpy, fixed seeds, 22,050 Hz
mono 16-bit, about 5.9 MB for 31 files, the three loops being 4.8 MB of it in `game/sounds/`).

**Web (fixed 2026-10-22).** The buses are made with `AudioServer.bus_count = n`, never `add_bus()`. On the web, Godot 4.7 keeps its own copy of the bus list for Sample playback, and `add_bus()` puts the new bus in the wrong place there (`Bus.addAt(-1)` splices it before the last bus); the next `set_bus_send` then sends Master into a child bus and nothing reaches the speakers while the engine still reports every clip playing. The web keeps Sample playback: bus volumes and mute apply, bus effects do not, so clips play centred on the web (the pan buses only pan natively). `?audio-debug=1` shows a label (playback type, context state, last clip playing), prints AUDIODEBUG lines and plays a test horn on the first input; `server/cmd/webcheck` (audio.go) checks a desktop click and a phone tap in a browser without the autoplay flag, with a non-silent signal measured at the context's destination.
